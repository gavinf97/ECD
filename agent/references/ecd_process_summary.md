# ECD Process V1 — condensed summary

**Source:** Farrell G. et al., *ELIXIR Community Databases (ECD) Process – V1* (July 2025),
[doi:10.5281/zenodo.17288943](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17288943), CC BY 4.0.
**Companion form:** *ECD Checklist – V1*, [doi:10.5281/zenodo.17289009](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17289009).
The full extracted texts are in `ecd_process_v1_text.txt` and `ecd_checklist_v1_text.txt`.

## Purpose

- **Who it's for:** biodata resources on the ELIXIR Service Delivery Plan (SDP) that serve narrower
  scientific Communities, and are not, or may never be, Core Data Resources (CDR) or ELIXIR
  Deposition Databases (EDD).
- **Goals:**
  - stronger links between resources and Communities
  - Community-driven co-development
  - more visibility
  - a formal "ELIXIR Community Database" status
- **Numbers (July 2025):** 187 SDP data resources, 32 CDR, 16 EDD.

## Six steps

1. **SDP engagement.** Nodes hear about ECD, CDR/EDD, GCBR, CoreTrustSeal and TTRAM during the annual SDP update.
2. **Self-assessment checklist.** The applicant completes *every* field and applies to **one** Community.
   It emails the PDF to `elixir-community-databases@elixir-europe.org`.
3. **Hub eligibility check.** Hub staff check that:
   - it is a deposition database, knowledgebase, registry or aggregation database
   - it is for European or international use
   - it comes from an ELIXIR Node institute
   - it is on a Node SDP
   - it is not already CDR, EDD, GCBR or RIR
   - all sections are complete
4. **Community & Data Platform joint decision.**
   - Community co-leads and Data Platform ExCo decide by consensus within 3 months.
   - Conflicts of interest are delegated.
   - Feedback goes on the submitted form.
   - After a rejection, reapplication is possible after 12 months, with changes shown in Appendix 1.
5. **One-year health check.**
   - The resource gets provisional status and a banner.
   - The Hub monitors uptime against a ~99% target.
   - The Community sets up engagement channels (roadmap advice, interoperability/APIs, visibility).
   - Outcome: full ECD status for 3 years, or provisional status revoked.
6. **Monitoring & retention.** The resource resubmits the Checklist every 3 years and goes through the same review, with no further health check.

## Appendix 3 — rejection criteria (what reviewers look for)

**Administrative (Hub; immediate).** Any of these stops the application:
- wrong resource type
- no ELIXIR affiliation or SDP listing
- already a CDR, EDD or GCBR
- incomplete form

**Scientific & service level (Community + Data Platform; discretionary).** Grouped by area, with the Checklist questions each draws on:

| Area | What reviewers look for | Checklist questions |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance & uniqueness | lack of relevance to the Community; no identifiable gap, or redundant | S2.1a, S2.1c, S2.1d |
| Sustainability & governance | no staffing or hardware for the next ~3 years; critical dependence on one person; no roadmap, SAB or feedback mechanism | S4.1, S4.2, S4.5, S4.7, S4.8, S4.9 |
| Interoperability & data management | a data silo (no metadata standards or ontologies, and no API or machine access); restrictive or unclear licences; no long-term storage or off-site backups | S5.1–S5.4, S6.2, S6.3, S6.6 |

**Health check.** Validated uptime below 99% during the probation period.

**Using this in the skill.**
- **Scope:** the skill pre-fills the *applicant's* checklist; it does not judge the application.
- **Report gaps:** the final chat summary should still flag answers that map onto these rejection
  criteria, e.g. S1.4 "Not met", or no API plus no ontologies.
- **Why:** the applicant can see where the application is weak before submitting.
