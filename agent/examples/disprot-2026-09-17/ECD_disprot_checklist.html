Section 2: Resource Background

1. Biodata resource key info:
1.aGap your resource serves:
Inferred – reviewmedium confidence
Response · 100 words
Intrinsically disordered proteins and regions lack a fixed 3D structure, so they are poorly captured by structure-centred resources and cannot be characterised reliably by prediction alone. DisProt fills this gap as the reference, manually curated collection of experimentally validated disorder, linking each region to its detection method (ECO), structural state, function and transitions (IDPO, GO) and supporting publication. These gold-standard annotations underpin disorder predictor benchmarking (e.g. CAID) and are cross-referenced from UniProtKB.

  • Protein Ensemble Database (PED; conformational ensembles of IDPs)

State what your resource does better than resources serving the same gap · 100 words
DisProt is dedicated to manual, literature-based curation of experimentally validated disorder at region level, with every annotation tied to an ECO evidence code, IDPO/GO terms, MIADE information and a curator-review workflow, released twice a year with archived versions. MobiDB aggregates curated and predicted disorder, and PED stores structural ensembles rather than curated evidence.
